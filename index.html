01 / About

An artist.
A collaborator.
A builder.

My work lives between the rehearsal room and the structures that make creative exploration possible.

I am an interdisciplinary artist, choreographer, and arts administrator whose practice brings Afro-Caribbean movement into conversation with contemporary performance, storytelling, and visual media.

As co-founder and Artistic Director of FuturPointe Dance, I develop original work through collaboration, experimentation, and revision. My performance background includes Garth Fagan Dance, Dance Theatre of Harlem, and L’Acadco: A United Caribbean Dance Force.

At the University of Wisconsin–Madison, I coordinate artist residencies that connect creative practice with teaching, research, and community life—supporting both the artistic questions and the practical work behind them.

MBA, Rochester Institute of Technology · BFA in Dance, SUNY Brockport · Professional Diploma in Dance Theatre and Production, Edna Manley College, Jamaica

01 / PERFORMANCE

Psychopomp & Pageantry

Dance. Ritual. Magic. An interdisciplinary production connecting contemporary Caribbean movement, classical ballet, and folklore, presented in Rochester and at Let’s Dance International Dance Festival in Leicester, UK.

Creator / Director / Choreographer / Performer
02 / PERFORMANCE + INSTALLATION

Riot

A performance and installation commissioned by St. John Fisher College to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the 1964 Rochester riots—bringing performance into conversation with difficult local history.

Creator / Director
03 / MOVEMENT + TEXT

Dancing with Words

A workshop collaboration with Dr. Sharrell D. Luckett at the University of Cincinnati, exploring how movement can generate material for poetry, plays, monologues, and prose.

Co-Creator / Organizer / Curator
04 / RESIDENCY + DEVELOPMENT

CASBAR

Can A Song Be A Revolution? Supported developmental planning with Sri Vamsi Matta as the project evolved from a theatre concept toward interdisciplinary performance, helping articulate its artistic and educational direction.

Planning / Developmental Facilitation / Project Framing
05 / PRODUCTION + PARTNERSHIP

Come Eat With Me

Production leadership for Sri Vamsi Matta’s project across three Wisconsin engagements, bringing together fiscal stewardship, logistics, and the practical framework for public presentation.

Producer / Production Lead
06 / LIGHT + IMAGE

Water, Seeds and Soil

Lighting and projection design for choreographer David Blake’s work, presented at The Place in London and MAC Birmingham.

Lighting / Projection Design

03 / How I work

Serve the work.
Support the artist.

Creative partnership means listening closely, making room for uncertainty, and taking responsibility for the conditions that allow a work to develop.

01

Make & develop

Choreography, interdisciplinary performance, and collaborative processes that move from first questions through rehearsal, experimentation, and revision.

02

Connect & produce

Artist residencies, university-community partnerships, production planning, budgets, and teams that keep artistic ambition connected to practical delivery.

03

Teach & exchange

Movement workshops, creative-career conversations, and residency-design sessions that translate artistic practice into shared tools for discovery.

Start with what a work is trying to become. Then ask what will help it get there.
